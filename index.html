Why RelixZoon?

Our mission is to empower independent artists through direct audience relationships and more opportunities to monetise their creativity.

RelixZoon isn't a streaming service that pays per play. Fans pay artists directly, for the releases they unlock and the subscriptions they choose.

More independence
You decide how and when your music is released.
Direct relationships

Flexible release strategies
